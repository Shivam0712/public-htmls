How do the industry's numbered commissioning Levels differ from ASHRAE's commissioning phases, and what roles do ASHRAE Guideline 0 and Standard 202 play?

The industry’s Levels describe widening test scope, from factory-tested components (Level 1) to the whole plant under simulated failures (Level 5). ASHRAE instead organizes commissioning by pre-design, design, construction, and occupancy/operations phases. Standard 202 sets minimum process requirements; Guideline 0 lays out best-practice phases. Five Levels are conventional: programs may add Level 0 design review, Level 6 turnover, or Level 7 first-year check-in. Their correspondence to ASHRAE phases is approximate.
