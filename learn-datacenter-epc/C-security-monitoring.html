Why can ordinary spot smoke detectors respond late in a high-airflow data hall, and how does aspirating smoke detection improve early warning?

Fast cooling airflow can dilute smoke and carry it past ordinary spot detectors before enough reaches them to trigger an alarm. Aspirating smoke detection (ASD, including VESDA systems) instead continuously draws air through sampling pipes and can detect combustion products at concentrations roughly 100 times below conventional thresholds. EN 54-20 grades ASD sensitivity A, B or C; Class A is the most sensitive.

How do single- and double-interlock pre-action sprinklers differ in when water enters the piping, and why is reliable sprinkler fire control still the white-space baseline?

In a single-interlock system, detector activation admits water into the sprinkler piping; an opened sprinkler head is still needed for discharge. Double-interlock requires both detector activation and a head opening before water enters the piping. Only opened heads discharge. Pre-action sprinklers remain the US white-space baseline because reliable fire control takes priority over avoiding all possible water contact with equipment.

How does continuous oxygen reduction prevent ignition, and what room-sealing, occupancy and running-cost constraints limit its use?

Card 68 of 81interpretation

Why do critics say PUE misses useful computing work and other resource impacts, yet defenders still retain it as a common efficiency yardstick?

PUE compares total facility energy with IT energy, not the useful computing work produced. As AI increases IT power, PUE can miss whether that energy delivers more computation; it also says nothing about water use or carbon. Critics therefore want water, carbon and compute-productivity measures alongside it. Defenders retain PUE because it remains a comparable efficiency baseline with decades of history.
